SELLERPHOTO STUDIO PRO 1.1
==========================

SellerPhoto Studio Pro is a private, browser-based seller workspace with three paid tools:
1. Image Studio for marketplace and catalogue images
2. Video Studio for short product videos
3. Forecast Lab for product-lifetime revenue, profit and cash-flow planning

QUICK START
1. Extract the ZIP file to a folder.
2. Double-click index.html. Keep all six files together.
3. Use the tabs at the top to move between Image Studio, Video Studio and Forecast Lab.
4. Your media stays on this device. No product photo, logo, video scene or audio file is uploaded.

IMAGE STUDIO
- Add up to 50 JPG, PNG or WebP product photos, each 25 MB or smaller.
- Choose a conservative starting preset for Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, quick commerce, Shopify, Instagram, Reels/Stories or custom dimensions.
- Toggle background, fit/crop, spacing, tone corrections, shadow, rotation, price, watermark, logo, safe-zone and catalogue manifest controls independently.
- Compliance Guard forces a white canvas and suppresses promotional overlays for strict main-image presets.
- Review the six-point pre-upload inspector before export.
- Download an organised ZIP with optional SKU-friendly names and catalogue manifest CSV.

VIDEO STUDIO
- Add and arrange up to 12 product-photo scenes.
- Choose 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9 output.
- Set scene duration and 24 or 30 fps.
- Toggle motion, transitions, scene captions, price and call-to-action, logo, music and safe-zone guides independently.
- Click Generate product video to download a WebM file.
- Some seller or social portals require MP4. Convert the WebM export to MP4 only when the destination requires it.
- Video duration determines generation time; the browser renders the video in real time.

FORECAST LAB
- Model 12, 24, 36, 48 or 60 months.
- Set launch demand, ramp growth, maturity, decline, seasonality and peak month.
- Add selling price, annual price erosion, product cost, fulfilment, packaging and annual cost inflation.
- Include platform fees, advertising, returns or wastage, handling, launch cost, fixed overhead, estimated tax and inventory cover.
- Review lifetime revenue, lifetime net profit after launch cost, cash breakeven, ROI, peak month and target gap.
- Compare conservative, base and aggressive scenarios.
- Use the price-demand sensitivity grid, unit economics, decision signals and month-by-month table.
- Save assumptions locally, export CSV or print/save the report as PDF.

IMPORTANT PLATFORM NOTE
Marketplace, quick-commerce and social requirements vary by account, category and time. Presets and the inspector are practical starting points, not official certification. Always verify current image and video requirements inside the destination seller portal before publishing.

IMPORTANT FORECAST NOTE
Forecast results depend entirely on the assumptions entered. They do not guarantee sales, demand, profit or cash recovery and are not financial, tax or investment advice. Use conservative assumptions and replace them with actual operating data after launch.

SUPPORTED BROWSERS
- Microsoft Edge
- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari

For the most reliable video export, use a current version of Edge or Chrome. If opening index.html directly is restricted by a managed browser, place the folder on a local web server or try another supported browser.

TIPS
- Keep an uncropped, high-resolution source copy of every product photo.
- For strict marketplace main images, start with Compliance Guard on.
- Use owned-channel presets when adding price badges, branding or promotional overlays.
- JPG at 85–92% is a strong balance between quality and file size. PNG is better for graphics and flat artwork.
- Use short, stable SKU prefixes such as BRAND-CATEGORY-001.
- Use licensed or owned audio in product videos.
- Save a conservative forecast before scaling ads or buying a large inventory position.

LIMITS
- Up to 50 photos per image batch.
- Up to 12 scenes per video.
- Individual source images must be 25 MB or smaller.
- Video export is WebM and is rendered in real time.
- The image editor standardises the canvas; it does not remove backgrounds with AI.
- Saved preferences and forecasts use this browser's local storage.
