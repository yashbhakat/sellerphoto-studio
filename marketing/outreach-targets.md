## Qualification before contact

Contact only when at least one public signal is present: the business actively sells online, the person publicly discusses ecommerce operations, or the service provider handles catalogues. Personalise one sentence using a public business detail. Stop after one follow-up if there is no reply.

## Approval gate

Before any message is sent, record: recipient or community, exact final text, destination URL, sender identity, and whether the platform permits promotional links. The owner must approve the batch immediately before sending.
