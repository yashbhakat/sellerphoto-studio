## Full description

SellerPhoto Studio formats, brands, resizes and exports up to 50 product photos at once. It is designed for sellers operating on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Shopify, ONDC, Instagram, WhatsApp and their own sites or apps.

Choose a channel-oriented size, set Fit or Fill, select a background, add a store name, price badge or logo, and apply an SKU prefix. The finished ZIP can include a catalogue manifest CSV that maps every source image to its export filename, dimensions, format and SKU prefix.

Everything runs in the browser on the buyer’s device. Photos are not uploaded, there is no account to manage, and there are no AI or per-image usage fees.

## What it does not do

SellerPhoto Studio does not remove backgrounds with AI, publish listings directly, or quote official marketplace fees. It standardises the canvas and controls how the original photograph is positioned inside it. Sellers should check the current requirements in their own seller portals.

## Requirements

A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. No installation or account is needed.
