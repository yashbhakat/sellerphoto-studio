## Full description

SellerPhoto Studio formats, brands, resizes, and exports up to 50 product photos at once. It is made for small sellers who list products on Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart, Instagram, or WhatsApp and want a cleaner catalogue without repeating the same editing work.

Choose a marketplace size, set how the product fits, select a background, add your store name, price badge, or logo, and download the entire finished batch as one ZIP.

Everything runs in the browser on your device. Photos are not uploaded, there is no account to manage, and there are no AI or usage fees.

## What it does not do

SellerPhoto Studio does not remove backgrounds with AI. It standardises the canvas and controls how the original photograph is positioned inside it.
