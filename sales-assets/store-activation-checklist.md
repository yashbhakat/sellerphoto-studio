# Store activation checklist

## Lemon Squeezy

1. Create or sign in to your Lemon Squeezy account.
2. Complete store verification using your real legal and payout information.
3. If you do not have a Stripe-approved Indian bank payout account, connect PayPal for payouts.
4. Create a digital product named “SellerPhoto Studio — Full Offline Edition”.
5. Set the launch price to ₹499, or the closest supported localised price.
6. Upload `SellerPhotoStudio-v1.0.0.zip` as the product file.
7. Paste the copy from `product-listing.md` into the product page.
8. Add the product screenshots and short demo video.
9. Publish the product and copy its checkout URL.

## Connect the checkout

Set the hosted site variable `NEXT_PUBLIC_CHECKOUT_URL` to the published checkout URL, rebuild, and publish the new version. Every purchase can then deliver the ZIP automatically.

## Before sending traffic

- Make one real low-value test purchase or use the platform’s test mode.
- Confirm the receipt contains the download.
- Download and extract the ZIP on another device.
- Open `index.html`, process three photos, and confirm the result ZIP opens correctly.
- Check that your support email and refund wording are visible on the checkout page.
- Keep your own Indian income and tax records; merchant-of-record sales-tax handling does not replace your local reporting obligations.

## Minimal launch distribution

- Post the 30-second demo in three seller communities where promotional posts are permitted.
- Publish one before/after example on Instagram and WhatsApp Status.
- Offer the ₹499 price only to the first 20 customers.
- Ask buyers one question after purchase: “What would save you the most time in version 1.1?”
